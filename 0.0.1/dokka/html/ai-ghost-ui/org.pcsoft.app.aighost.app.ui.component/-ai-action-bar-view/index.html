AiActionBarView

class AiActionBarView : FxmlView<AiActionBarViewModel> , Initializable

View of AiActionBar, holding the three buttons of the floating AI action bar.

The bar is half-transparent while the mouse is elsewhere and fades to full opacity while the mouse sits directly over it, so it stays out of the way of reading without disappearing entirely. No button carries any logic beyond marking itself as not implemented (IP-18): the real actions are the job of a later feature, once lib/ai's action port is actually wired up.
